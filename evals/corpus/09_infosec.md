# Information Security Policy

Applies to every employee and contractor of the Koinoti pharmacy chain who uses a company computer, the cashier console or the inventory system.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and contain upper-case letters, lower-case letters and digits. Passwords are changed every 90 days. A password may not repeat any of the last five passwords. Never share a password, not even with your store manager.

## Access to systems

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for the cashier console and for remote access to the inventory system. Workstations lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. USB storage devices are disabled on all cashier computers.

## Incidents

Report any suspected security incident — a phishing email, a lost laptop, an unknown person at a terminal — to it-security@koinoti.tj within 1 hour of noticing it. Do not try to investigate on your own.
